5 FAMOUS INDIAN FOODS

INTRODUCTION

Indian cuisine is known for its rich flavors, spices, and variety. Each region of India has its own unique dishes and cooking styles. Popular foods include biryani, butter chicken, dosa, samosa, paneer tikka, chole bhature, and idli. Indian food often uses spices such as turmeric, cumin, cardamom, and cinnamon, making it flavorful and diverse.
