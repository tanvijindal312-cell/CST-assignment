INTRODUCTION

Chitkara University is one of the premier private universities in North India, widely recognized for its focus on multi-disciplinary, industry-relevant higher education. Founded by dedicated educators Dr. Ashok K. Chitkara and Dr. Madhu Chitkara, the university originally began its journey in 2002 as the Chitkara Institute of Engineering and Technology. It was formally established as a university in 2010 through the passing of "The Chitkara University Act" by the Punjab State Legislature.
